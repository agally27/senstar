# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions as ADRs

**Status:** Accepted (founder, 2026-08-14)
**Date:** 2026-08-14

## Decision

All significant architectural and infrastructure decisions are recorded as numbered ADRs in `docs/adr/`, using the 0000 template, and require founder approval before implementation. Founder-level open items are tracked in `DECISIONS.md`; an accepted ADR closes its D-number. Changing an accepted decision requires a superseding ADR — never a silent edit.

## Reason

Prompt 00 §8/§12 and the Technical Constitution require documented, non-silent decision-making; this ADR makes the mechanism itself official.

## Alternatives

Ad-hoc decision notes (rejected: unfindable, unversioned); decisions only in DECISIONS.md (rejected: no room for alternatives/consequences analysis).

## Security & child-data implications

Indirect but real: the mandatory "child-data implications" section in every ADR forces the residency/processor assessment at decision time.

## Outcome

Accepted by founder, 2026-08-14.
